Claim wording

Claim for Trip Delay and Trip Interruption, Aruba trip, November 2026. Insureds: Rob [last name] and Amanda [last name]. Certificate number [number].

Our JetBlue flight from Aruba (AUA) to Pittsburgh (PIT) on Saturday, November 14, 2026 was cancelled following the nationwide FAA ground stop on November 13, as documented in the attached JetBlue letter dated [letter date]. JetBlue’s next available flight was Thursday, November 19.

Our original Divi Golf Resort reservation ended on November 14. Because we could not travel home, we checked out of our original reservation and booked a new four-night reservation at the same hotel, at the Divi’s all-inclusive rate of $3,633.92, which includes meals. The only alternative we found was Embassy Suites at $2,464.81 for the same dates, without meals (quote attached). JetBlue did not offer us any hotel or meal vouchers.

American Airlines had availability on Wednesday, November 18 at roughly the same cost, so we booked those flights instead of waiting for JetBlue on the 19th. That shortened our delay by a day. The two American tickets cost $772.08, and JetBlue refunded the original tickets for $646.36. We also paid [$____] for additional airport parking (receipts attached).

We are requesting (1) Trip Delay reimbursement for lodging and meals, (2) Trip Interruption reimbursement of additional transportation cost, $772.08 less $646.36, or $125.72, and (3) consideration of the parking expense. All receipts are attached. Please tell us if you need anything further.

Watch-outs

  • The IMG delay benefit says the delay must prevent you from staying at the originally booked accommodations. A new reservation at the same resort is a gray area. The sample also extends coverage while an unavoidable delay keeps you from returning, which supports you.
  • The airfare benefit depends on IMG treating a federal FAA ground stop as a government-mandated air traffic shutdown. The sample says “local government,” so expect pushback.
  • Answer any question on the claim form about other insurance accurately. The certificate can void coverage for a concealed material fact.
  • If IMG denies any part, ask for the denial in writing with the exact provision.
  • Your own certificate and state endorsement control. This page summarizes a sample policy.
